# Privacy Policy — Tidyups Cleaning Service Inc.

_Last updated: June 21, 2026_

Tidyups Cleaning Service Inc. ("Tidyups", "we", "us") operates the Tidyups Cleaning mobile app and
website. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

## Information we collect

**Quote requests (customers).** When you request a quote we collect the details you provide: name,
phone number, and optionally email, service address, property details, preferred date, and message.
We use this information solely to prepare your quote, contact you about your booking, and deliver
our cleaning services.

**Staff location (employees only).** Cleaners on our team may choose to share their live location
with dispatch while travelling to a job site, using the "Cleaner Check-In" feature. Location sharing
is started and stopped by the staff member, is only collected while sharing is active, and is used
solely for dispatch and scheduling coordination. Customers are never location-tracked.

**Device storage.** The app stores small amounts of data on your device (such as your last quote
details, so you can re-book faster). This data stays on your device and can be cleared by deleting
the app.

## What we don't do
- We do not sell or rent your personal information.
- We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.
- We do not collect data from customers' devices in the background.

## Sharing
Your information is shared only with the Tidyups team members who prepare your quote and perform
your cleaning, and with the service providers that power our systems (secure cloud hosting and SMS
delivery). These providers may process data only on our behalf.

## Retention & your rights
We keep quote information for as long as needed to serve you and meet legal requirements. You may
ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time.
